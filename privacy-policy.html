Privacy Policy

How this website handles contact information, external services and privacy questions.

Last updated 28 October 2026

Information you share

When you contact Rebuilt Techz by email, telephone or WhatsApp, you may share your name, contact details, service requirements and project information. Please share only information needed to discuss your enquiry and avoid sending passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information.

Contact form and newsletter

The current website contact form checks your entries in your browser. It does not send your enquiry to Rebuilt Techz or save it in a website database. To send an enquiry, use the email, telephone or WhatsApp links on our Contact page. Newsletter subscriptions are currently unavailable.

Enquiries and correspondence

Information you send through our contact channels is used to understand your requirements, respond to your questions and discuss requested services. Email, telephone and messaging providers handle communications under their own privacy practices. Contact us if you have questions about information shared in your enquiry.

External services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from the cdnjs content delivery network. The Contact page also embeds Google Maps. Your browser connects to these providers to load their content, which can disclose technical information such as your IP address and browser details.

WhatsApp and map links open external services. Their privacy policies apply when you use them. See Google Privacy Policy, Google Fonts privacy information, Cloudflare Privacy Policy and WhatsApp Privacy Policy.

Cookies and technical data

The website application does not currently set its own analytics or advertising cookies or store form entries in browser storage. Embedded services may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. Your browser settings let you manage cookies. Website hosting providers may process technical request data to deliver and secure the site.

Questions and information requests

To ask about information you have shared, request a correction or request deletion of enquiry correspondence, email info@rebuilttechz.com. Include enough context to identify your enquiry without sending unnecessary sensitive information. Any applicable recordkeeping requirements may affect what can be deleted.

Updates to this policy

This page describes the website features currently available. We will update it when our contact form, subscriptions or other data handling features change. The latest revision date appears above.
